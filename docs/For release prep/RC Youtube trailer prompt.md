# RC Youtube trailer prompt

Lets make a youtube trailer for this program. FFMPEG is available via Windows PATH for your use.

I will provide a music track - use it from start and if the video ends before the music ends, then just fade out the music, do not try to splice it to match the music end to video end - keep it simple in this regard.

At the end, update promo.md with title, description and tags that I will paste into youtube.

Final render should be 16:9, 1080p, no larger. 

If applicable, do a Youtube Shorts version of the trailer in 9:16 aspect ratio.

If a particular video is long, experimental, or were somewhat unsure about its direction, then you can render it at a lower resolution like 480p initially, then upgrade to 1080p for the final render.

You've been amazingly good at making youtube trailers for me, so I generally trust your process where you usually analyze the music track to figure out the pacing, then draft a shots list plan into a trailer.md file, then I approve it and you do it mostly hands free.

## Loose reference for guidance

Note: whatever I say in the prompt will override the below stuff. I may give style/pacing guidance or leave it to the music track to set the pace - usually I'll hand-pick a track that nails the mood/feel I'm going for.

### Lenght
Small tool: 30 secs
Small game or medium tool: 60-90 secs
Upper limit: 4 minutes

### Style
Options: Kinetic text animation (but not too obnoxious to make the smallest of text jump/dance to *every* beat - avoid that, broader strokes is better), screenshot or promo/ or assets/ folder based, capture footage or stills that are animated in sequence, or a mix of any of those. Choose whatever suits the subject/project/game/software in question.
