# Wiggle My Image Animator — Guide 🍋

## TL;DR

1. Open `index.html` (or the itch.io page).
2. Drop a picture onto the page. It starts wiggling right away.
3. Pick a style, play with the sliders, or hit 🎲 Surprise me.
4. Hit 💾 Download GIF. Done!

## Getting a picture in

- Click **📂 Choose a picture**, drag a picture anywhere onto the page, or paste one with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
- Most picture types work: PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG. For animated GIFs only the first frame is used.
- Pictures with a see-through background (like PNG stickers or cut-outs) look best, because then only the subject wiggles.
- iPhone HEIC photos only open in Safari. In other browsers you'll get a friendly message; save the photo as JPG first.

## Making it wiggle

- **Wiggle style:** each button shows the path the wiggle travels along. Breathe is different: it puffs the middle of the picture in and out.
- **Wiggliness:** how twisty it gets.
- **Wander:** how far the wiggle roams across the picture.
- **Speed:** how fast one loop is (shown in seconds).
- **🎲 Surprise me:** random style and settings.
- **Reverse:** runs the loop backwards.
- **Show path:** draws the path on the picture (only in the preview, never in your saved file).
- **Stay in frame:** zooms the picture out just enough that no part of it ever wiggles off the edge.
- **Background:** see-through, white, black, green screen, magenta, or tap the rainbow dot to pick any colour. Green screen and magenta are handy for cutting the wiggle out in video editors.
- **Peek:** hold down on the picture (or click it and hold Space) to see the original.

## Saving and sharing

- Pick a size first: **Small**, **Medium** or **Large** (320, 480 or 720 pixels on the long side). The exact size is shown under the buttons.
- **💾 Download GIF:** a GIF that loops forever. Best for chats, memes, forums and stickers. With the see-through background the GIF stays see-through, but its edges get a bit jagged (that's GIFs for you).
- **🎬 Download video:** a short clip of a few whole loops, as MP4 or WebM depending on your browser. Handy where GIFs aren't allowed. Videos can't be see-through, so they get a white background instead. Videos record in real time, so keep the tab open until it's done.
- **📤 Share** (phones, and some computers): sends the GIF straight to your apps. If your phone says "Ready! Tap to share", just tap it once more.
- Files are named after your picture, like `cat_wiggle.gif`.
- **Cancel** stops a save that's in progress.

## Tips

- Faces and round things look great with Circle or Breathe at high Wiggliness.
- Low Wiggliness plus high Wander gives a gentle, wobbly "jelly" look.
- Large GIFs of busy photos can be a few megabytes. If a chat app refuses it, try Small or Medium.
- Your settings are remembered the next time you open the app (in the same browser).
- Light or dark? The app follows your device's setting until you tap the 🌙/☀️ button at the top, which flips it and remembers your choice.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your pictures are never uploaded anywhere.
