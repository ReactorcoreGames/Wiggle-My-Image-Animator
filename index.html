How to wiggle 🍋

  1. Pick a picture. Tap Choose a picture, drag one onto the page, or paste one with Ctrl+V.
  2. Pick a wiggle style. Each little drawing shows the path the wiggle travels along.
  3. Tweak it. Wiggliness is how twisty it gets, Wander is how far it roams, Speed is how fast it loops.
  4. Can't decide? Smash 🎲 Surprise me.
  5. Picture wiggling off the edge? Tick Stay in frame and it zooms to fit the whole wiggle. Tap the rainbow dot to pick any background colour.
  6. Hold down on the picture (or click it and hold Space) to compare with the original.
  7. Save it. Pick a size, then hit Download GIF. It loops forever, so it's perfect for chats and posts. Download video records a short clip instead (handy where GIFs aren't welcome). On phones, Share sends the GIF straight to your apps.

With the see-through background, GIFs keep their transparency (edges get a little jagged, that's GIFs for you). Videos can't be see-through, so they get a white background. Videos record in real time (a few seconds), so keep this tab open until it's done.

Everything happens right here in your browser. Your pictures never leave your device.
